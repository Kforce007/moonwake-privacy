Moonwake

Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 Developer: Qobly App: Moonwake (Android package com.moonwake.game)
Plain-language summary

Your game stays on your phone. Only our ad partners receive data, and only to show ads.

Moonwake has no account and no server of its own. It is free because it shows ads, delivered by Unity. Here's exactly what that means.

  • Your progress, settings and year of birth are stored only on your device. Qobly never receives them.
  • Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay by ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, which receive device information such as your advertising ID and IP address in order to show and measure ads.
  • You decide whether ads may be personalised, and can change your mind any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • Reminders are written and scheduled on your phone, never sent from a server, and you can switch them off any time.
  • No account, no sign-in, no name or email collected, no in-app purchases.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or GPS location. The app never asks for these.

01 What this covers

This policy describes how Moonwake, a calm river game made by Qobly, handles information when you play it on Android. It covers the app itself, the advertising inside it and the Google Play features it uses. It doesn't cover other Qobly products or websites you may reach from an ad.

Moonwake has no server component of its own. The rivers, music and artwork are all built into the app, and the game plays fully offline. The only parts of the app that use the internet are the advertising services described in section 3 and Google Play's rating card described in section 7.

02 What's stored on your device

Moonwake keeps a small amount of information on your phone using Android's standard app storage. None of it is sent to Qobly or to anyone else, and all of it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

03 Advertising

Moonwake is free to play and supported by ads. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay, a mediation service from Unity Technologies, which runs a short auction between the ad networks it works with, currently ironSource Ads (Unity's own network) and Unity Ads. Ads appear only at natural breaks, after a voyage or on the way back to the menu, or when you choose to watch one for a reward. They never interrupt play.

When the game requests or shows an ad, these partners collect information directly from your device. Qobly does not receive this information.

04 Your ad choices

  • Personalised ads: turn them on or off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Personalised ads. When they are off you still see ads, and the same number of them, but they are not based on a profile of you.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the first time you play, Moonwake asks whether ads may be personalised. Personalisation stays off unless you choose to allow it. "Allow" and "Don't allow" are offered equally.
  • United States and other regions: switching personalised ads off is our "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" option. It tells our partners not to use your data for targeted advertising.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by phone).
  • Android ad privacy: on phones that have it, Settings → Privacy → Ads → Ad privacy lets you switch off ad topics, app-suggested ads and ad measurement for every app at once.

Your choice is passed to Unity LevelPlay before any ad is requested, and again whenever you change it. LevelPlay forwards it to every ad network it works with.

05 Age and children

Moonwake is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children. Children's privacy laws require ads to be handled differently for younger players, so on first launch the game asks for your year of birth, without suggesting any particular answer. It's stored only on your device and is never sent to anyone; only the resulting setting (for example, "treat as a child") is passed to our ad partners.

  • If you are under 13, our ad partners are told to treat you as a child under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): ads are never personalised and no behavioural profile is built.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, ads are never personalised for anyone under 16.
  • The year of birth can't be changed afterwards, and "Reset progress" keeps it, so these protections can't be switched off from inside the game.

If you believe a child has used Moonwake and that data about them was collected, please contact us. We'll help you contact Unity to have it removed.

06 Reminders and notifications

Moonwake can send two kinds of gentle reminder. Both are written and scheduled on your phone itself: there is no notification server, and Qobly never knows whether you have reminders on or what they say.

  • Daily river: one note a day, at a time you choose (8 pm unless you change it), when the new daily river opens.
  • Gentle reminders: an occasional note if you've been away for a while (after about 3, 7 and 14 days). Opening the game resets that clock.

To make them less repetitive, the text can mention the day's river and your own progress, such as your best score or how many items you have left to find. That information is read from your phone's storage at the moment the reminder is scheduled and never leaves your device.

The first time you open Moonwake, Android asks whether it may send notifications. If you allow it, both reminders are on. If you don't, or close the question without answering, the game may ask once more on a later day and then not again. You can switch each reminder on or off at any time in Settings → Reminders, or turn off Moonwake's notifications entirely in Android's settings.

07 Ratings on Google Play

Occasionally, after a voyage that went well and only once you've played for a while, Moonwake may show Google Play's own rating card, so you can rate the game without leaving it. Settings also has a "Rate Moonwake" link that opens the game's Play Store page.

The rating card is provided and handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. Moonwake never sees whether you rated it, your rating or your review, and rating is always optional: nothing in the game changes either way.

09 What we don't collect

To be specific rather than vague about it, Moonwake does not:

  • Ask you to create an account, sign in, or give your name, email address or phone number.
  • Send your game progress, settings, year of birth or play statistics to Qobly or anyone else.
  • Use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own. The play statistics in the Journal are counted on your device and stay there.
  • Access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • Offer in-app purchases or collect payment information.

10 Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and U.S. state privacy laws (for example, California's CCPA/CPRA) give you rights over your personal information. These include the right to access, correct or delete it; to object to or restrict its use; to withdraw consent; and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. You will not be treated differently for using any of these rights.

Where the GDPR applies, personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings → Privacy. Our partners' handling of the information they need to deliver and measure non-personalised ads and to prevent fraud is explained in their own privacy policy.

Because Qobly holds no personal information about you, requests about advertising data are best sent to Unity using the link in section 3. We're always happy to help: write to us at the address below. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

11 Retention and deletion

  • On your device: kept until you uninstall Moonwake or clear its storage in Android settings, which deletes everything. "Reset progress" in Settings clears your game progress but keeps your privacy and reminder settings.
  • With our ad partners: kept for as long as their privacy policy describes. You can limit it by switching personalised ads off and by resetting or deleting your advertising ID.

There is no Moonwake account, so there is no account to close and no server-side copy held by Qobly to request deletion of.

12 Security and international transfers

Information sent to our ad partners is encrypted in transit. Our partners may process it in countries other than your own, including the United States, using the safeguards described in their privacy policy.

13 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and inside the game, and the effective date above will be updated. If a change affects how ad partners may use your information, the game will ask for your choice again where the law requires it.
